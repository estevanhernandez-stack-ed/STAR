Hand your treatment to the department

A logline works; a paragraph works better. Era, place, and what your characters actually do is what turns a guess into a grounded fact.

Your treatment itself is not stored — only the profile the department extracts from it, and the research it produces. Both are kept under this browser's identity, so you can come back to them. Nothing is visible without your sign-in token.

Four drawers come back

  • Setting & Atmosphere Light, sound, weather, dress, money, food — the texture of a place in its period.
  • Objects & Props What characters hold, drive and wear: makes, models, materials, and the era's own words for them.
  • Logistics How people and things move: routes, distances, travel times, schedules, procedures.
  • Forces & Conflicts What stands in the way: institutions, authorities, rivals, laws, and how they actually operated.

Every fact is filed with the sources that came back for it, the day they were retrieved, and each source's own excerpt where there was one. A cited link that never came back from a search is stamped, not dropped.
