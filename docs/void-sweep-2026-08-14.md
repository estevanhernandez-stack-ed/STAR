## The finding that matters is one decision, not twenty-six errors

**Eleven anachronisms trace to a single timeline choice, and they touch 18 of
the 31 scenes:**

| what | when it actually existed |
|---|---|
| Kaiserkeller — **10 scenes** | opened 14 October 1959 |
| Mach Schau — 3 scenes | Hamburg audiences, from 1960 |
| Top Ten Club (+ its neon sign, + the streets between it and the Kaiserkeller) | opened 31 October 1960 |
| Indra residency | from 17 August 1960 |
| eight hours a night, black leather, Preludin nights | the 1960–61 residencies |
| Premier kit in Blue Sparkle | not the drummers' kit in 1958–59 |

The draft places the Hamburg material in 1958–59. The Beatles reached Hamburg
in **August 1960**. That is not eleven mistakes; it is one decision, reported
eleven times, across more than half the book.

**And it may be the plot.** This is a Doctor Who story about a stolen chord and
a discord depot. If the timeline is meant to be wrong, the department has just
proved that an attentive reader would catch it in eighteen places — which is
the more useful answer of the two. The tool cannot tell those apart. The writer
can.

## A second cluster, four claims, one afternoon

The Casbah opened 29 August 1959, and the draft has it in 1958 — along with

one date.

## The catches that show the tool can do expert work

- **"He was seventeen."** George was 15 in 1958 and turned 17 in February 1960.
  Caught again in scene 19 — *"fifteen years old in Liverpool, seventeen now"* —
  against 1959, where he was 16. The arithmetic is right both times.
- **"Beatles" in scene 30.** They were the Quarrymen in 1959. That is the catch
  a Beatles expert makes and nobody else does.
- **Rory Storm and the Hurricanes**, three scenes: the band was Al Caldwell's
  Texans until late 1959.
- **'Twist and Shout'** (1961), **'Some Other Guy'** (1962), **Candid Camera**
  (UK debut September 1960), **Rickenbacker** (Lennon's, November 1960).
- **"turning it up to eleven"** — Spinal Tap, 1984. Correct for the third
  independent time this week.
